Fonts used by the Habitat themes

Figtree

The figtree-latin.woff2 and figtree-latin-ext.woff2 files are the Figtree
variable font by Erik Kennedy (https://github.com/erikdkennedy/figtree), as
subset for the web by Google Fonts, included unmodified.
The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full license is
in OFL-Figtree.txt.

Bricolage Grotesque

The bricolage-grotesque-latin.woff2 and bricolage-grotesque-latin-ext.woff2
files are the Bricolage Grotesque variable font by Mathieu Triay
(https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage), as subset for the web by Google
Fonts, included unmodified.
The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full license is
in OFL-BricolageGrotesque.txt.

SIL Open Font License: https://scripts.sil.org/OFL
